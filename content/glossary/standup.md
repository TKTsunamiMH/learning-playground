## In short
A standup is a short daily sync where teammates share progress, plans, and blockers.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
You quickly align on backend/frontend status and deployment risks.  
Mentioning blockers early helps prevent delays in reviews, testing, or infra setup.  
Good standups stay concise and action-focused.

## Example
Scenario: “Yesterday I finished API auth; today I wire UI; blocker is missing test data.”
