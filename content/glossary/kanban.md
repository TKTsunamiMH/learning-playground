## In short
Kanban is a visual workflow method that manages work items through columns and limits work in progress.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
You can track backend, frontend, and testing tasks by stage from “To Do” to “Done.”  
WIP limits help teams focus and reduce context switching.  
Kanban works well for continuous delivery without fixed-length sprints.

## Example
Scenario: Move card from “In Review” to “Done” after PR approval and deploy.
