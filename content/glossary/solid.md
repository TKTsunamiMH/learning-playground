## In short
SOLID is a set of five design principles for building maintainable and extensible object-oriented code.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
SOLID helps structure services, controllers, and domain classes with clearer responsibilities.  
It often improves testability and long-term changeability in C# codebases.  
Use it as guidance, not strict dogma, to avoid over-engineering.
