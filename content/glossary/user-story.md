## In short
A user story is a short requirement written from the user’s perspective.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
Stories translate product needs into implementable backend and frontend tasks.  
Acceptance criteria define what “done” means for API, UI, and tests.  
Clear stories reduce rework and misalignment during implementation.

## Example
Scenario: “As a user, I want to reset my password so I can regain account access.”
