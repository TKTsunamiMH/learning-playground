## In short
Agile is an iterative approach where teams deliver software in small, frequent increments.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
You break features into manageable tasks and gather feedback early.  
Backend, frontend, and deployment work are planned together in short cycles.  
This supports continuous improvement and reduces late surprises.

## Example
Scenario: Deliver login MVP this sprint, then add social login next sprint.
