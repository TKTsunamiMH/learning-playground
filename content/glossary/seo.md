## In short
SEO is the practice of improving how easily search engines can find and understand your pages.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
Technical SEO includes semantic HTML, clean metadata, and fast page delivery.  
If you build SPA-heavy apps, SSR or prerendering may improve indexing.  
Backend choices like routing and status codes also affect crawl behavior.

## Example
Scenario: Ensure pages return proper `<title>` tags and 404 status codes when missing.
