## In short
AI is software that performs tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as language understanding and prediction.

## What it means for a .NET Full Stack developer
You can integrate AI features like text assistance, summarization, and semantic search into apps.  
In .NET, this often means calling model APIs and handling prompts, tokens, and safety constraints.  
Practical AI work includes UX design, validation, and monitoring quality.
